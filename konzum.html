Helping people find their way around a supermarket.

A CX research and app concept for Konzum, part of Fortenova Group: how can shoppers with visual impairments, older shoppers and anyone who struggles to orient themselves find products in store on their own?

ClientKonzum app · Fortenova Group
RoleUX/UI
ContextFaculty of Graphic Arts
ServicesField observations · Customer interviews · Customer survey · Scientific topic research · Analysis · Prototype

Problem and goals

People with disabilities find it hard to navigate Konzum stores and find items inside them.

Goals

  • Speed up and ease navigation inside Konzum stores
  • Simplify finding the product you came for

Anticipated challenges

  • Technology limits and the cost of implementing it
  • Initial distrust and the time it takes to adapt to an app
  • Mapping where products sit in a store
  • Frequent rearrangement of items

Four stages

Research
Analysis
Ideation
Design

Who we designed for

Three groups: people with visual impairments, older people, and people who find it hard to get their bearings in a space.

60%of respondents prefer shopping in the store
73%think on-site assistance would make supermarket shopping easier
62%use a phone while shopping in store
86%do not use Konzum's app services

What the research told us

  • Most respondents use their phone while shopping
  • Products are easier to assess in person
  • Finding items is hard without extra assistance
  • Digital product descriptions are often not enough
  • Availability information matters
  • Some users distrust online payment

User challenges

  • In-store signage is inadequate
  • Telling products in the same category apart
  • Orientation, especially after items are moved
  • No personal assistant available at every moment

User needs

  • Find the items they want quickly and efficiently
  • Move around the store independently
  • A simple user experience
  • Easy access to product information

Current state in stores

Stores have tried several different approaches to signage, which points to a need for standardisation.

SWOT analysis

Strengths

Well-known Croatian brands, wide assortment, weekly deals, accessible prices

Weaknesses

Nearby competitors, poor in-store signage, hard-to-reach parking, unintuitive design

Opportunities

Better design, easy-to-read signage, simple UX, more detailed product descriptions

Threats

Security risks, staff shortages, inaccessible product placement, narrow aisles

The concept

Electronic price labels become the bridge between shelf and phone. Scanning a QR code or tapping an NFC tag on the label opens product information in the app. In-store WiFi lets the app locate the product and show the shopper where to go.
